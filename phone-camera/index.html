CamOrder Camera

From your device
to your session.

  1. Add to your Home Screen

    Open this page in Safari on the iPhone or iPad. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App if shown, then open its new icon. This removes Safari’s toolbar. No developer account or weekly reinstall.

  2. Frame the shot

    Tap Start camera and allow camera access. Choose front or back, rotate to landscape, then tap Clean view. Tap anywhere to bring the controls back. Keep the device unlocked; use a Focus to prevent notifications appearing in the capture.

  3. Connect over USB

    Use a data-capable cable and tap Trust if asked.

  4. Disable the device’s USB audio on the Mac

    Open Audio MIDI Setup in Applications → Utilities, then Window → Show Audio Devices. For this USB screen-capture setup, leave each iPhone/iPad disabled as an audio device. Click Disable if enabled; if you see Enable, it is already disabled. Use your usual audio interface for Logic.

  5. Select the screen input in CamOrder

    Refresh Live Inputs and select this iPhone or iPad’s screen input. Your iPhone’s Continuity Camera can appear separately. Check Live Inputs, assign the source to a lane, then arm and record in Logic.

If the device doesn’t appear

Unlock it, reconnect the cable, and refresh CamOrder’s input list. To test separately, open QuickTime Player → File → New Movie Recording on the Mac and choose this device from the menu beside Record. You should see its screen; you don’t need to record.

Close QuickTime’s preview before trying the direct input again. As a fallback in CamOrder 0.9.2 on macOS 14+, select Window (choose…) → Choose window… and share the QuickTime preview window.

What gets captured?

This app shows a local camera preview. The Mac captures the device’s screen over USB, so visible controls, system indicators, notifications, cropping and mirroring can appear in the output. It cannot detect the cable or tell you whether CamOrder is recording. Check the Mac’s preview before a take.

Quality is a request, not a guarantee. The screen’s size and the USB capture path also affect the final image. For audio/video alignment, record a clap and adjust the lane’s Sync offset in CamOrder.

Offline and keep-awake

Once the app reports Offline ready below, its camera page can reopen without internet. No video leaves the page over the network. Browser storage clearing or eviction can require another online visit. There is no app-signing expiry.

Keep-awake support varies. If the status asks you to, temporarily set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never and restore your usual setting afterward. Leaving the app or locking the screen can interrupt the camera.
