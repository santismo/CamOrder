You’re ready to make a video.

  1. Fully quit and reopen Logic Pro.
  2. On Stereo Out, choose Audio FX → Audio Units → Santismo → CamOrder Studio → Stereo.
  3. Create a CamOrder project, choose a camera or screen, and arm a video lane.
  4. Press Play or Record in Logic to capture. Stop, edit and export your movie.

If CamOrder is missing, rescan it in Logic’s Plug-in Manager.
